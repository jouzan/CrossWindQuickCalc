THE WIND COMPONENT GUIDE

A clearer picture of your approach.

Enter an ICAO airport code and a flight window in your selected time zone. Dates convert to UTC before selecting forecasts. PST is fixed UTC−08:00; MST is fixed UTC−07:00. Choose PDT or MDT separately when daylight time applies.

Crosswind = wind speed × |sin(wind − runway)|
Headwind = wind speed × cos(wind − runway)

Wind and runway headings are true. Negative headwind means tailwind. Each runway direction uses the forecast case with the highest gust crosswind; the sustained crosswind and headwind come from that same case. Variable winds show full-speed crosswind and tailwind bounds separately, not simultaneous components.

TAF is decoded by IEM. Prevailing groups extend to the next prevailing group or forecast expiry. Conditional groups are included using decoded start and end times, or conservatively to expiry when no end is provided. Groups without decoded wind speed are omitted. MOS points are not interpolated. METAR is an observation, not a forecast. TAF/MOS coverage varies by station; missing data is shown explicitly.

Aircraft profiles compare the worst gust crosswind (or sustained wind when no gust is available) with one identified public POH/AFM edition. Maximum demonstrated crosswind is a certification reference, not proof of capability or an operating clearance. The legacy RV-12 profile is a maximum from its limitations section. Other variants, serial numbers, flaps configurations, manual revisions, and supplements may differ. Verify your own approved manual. Tailwinds are separate from this crosswind comparison.

The red airplane marks a wind-only suggestion. Across all forecast groups in the selected window, directions with any gust tailwind are excluded; remaining directions are ranked by lowest worst-case gust crosswind, then strongest minimum headwind. Equal directions are listed together; one gets the marker. No marker is shown for calm winds, variable winds, or a window where every direction has a tailwind at some point. This does not check aircraft limits, ATC runway assignment, traffic, or temporary closures.

OurAirports supplies runway alignments and closed flags. Those flags are not live NOTAMs; temporary closures may be missing. The diagram is schematic. Missing runway alignments require a verified true heading entered in manual mode. This tool does not determine runway suitability.
